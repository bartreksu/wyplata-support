Privacy Policy — Talvo Your privacy matters to us. The app is designed to run entirely on your device. WHAT DATA WE PROCESS • Data about working time, rates, bonuses, tips, leave and schedule that you enter yourself. • Data is stored locally on your device, and if you enable sync — in your private iCloud account (Apple). WHAT WE DON'T DO • We do not collect, sell or share your data with third parties. • We do not require creating an account or logging in. • We do not use ads or tracking tools. • We do not send your data to our servers — we have no servers that process your data. iCLOUD • When sync is enabled, data is stored in your iCloud account and is subject to Apple's privacy policy. You can turn sync off in the app settings. DELETING DATA • You can delete all data at any time: Settings → "Delete my data". This erases the data from the device and from iCloud (if it was used).
